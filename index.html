How it works

From free design to forever lit in 1–2 days.

Free design & quote

Tell us about your home and we design the lighting plan for your exact roofline: peaks, eaves, and columns. Many homes we can price from aerial imagery; for the rest we come take a quick look. Either way, the quote is free and you approve everything before work starts.

Professional install

Our install team mounts slim aluminum track color-matched to your trim from 19 colors, tucked under the drip edge with no visible wires. Most homes take 1 to 2 days, and we leave the property spotless.

We connect the free app, build your first scenes and schedules, and show you everything before we leave the driveway. Set it once and your home lights itself every night.

FAQ

Asked and answered.

Can you see the lights during the day?

Barely. The low-profile aluminum track is color-matched to your roofline from 19 colors and tucks under the drip edge. From the street your home looks unchanged until dark.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your roofline length and design. Most homeowners are surprised how it compares to just a few seasons of paying for install and takedown. The quote is free and takes about a minute to request. We price many homes from aerial imagery and come take a quick look at the rest.

How long do the lights last?

Commercial-grade LEDs rated for 50,000+ hours, which is decades of normal use, sealed against sun, rain, and snow, and covered by a 5-year manufacturer warranty.

Is the app hard to use?

If you can send a text, you can run your lights. Pick a color, tap a preset, or set a schedule once. We set up your first scenes before we leave, and the free app has a demo mode you can try today.

Who does the installation?

Lumen 514 runs every job with an install team certified on the Bosso system. No visible wires, no mess left behind, and we never penetrate your roof.

What if something breaks?

Most issues are fixed remotely through the app's cloud access. Hardware is warrantied and serviceable by section, and you deal directly with the owner.
