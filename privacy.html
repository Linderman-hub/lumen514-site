Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026

Lumen 514 LLC ("Lumen 514," "we," "us") operates lumen514.com. This page explains what we collect when you visit or request a quote, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Quote requests. When you fill out our quote form, we collect the information you enter: your name, phone number, email, street address, and any notes about your home. We use it to design and price your lighting, contact you about your quote, and schedule work you hire us for.
  • Site usage. Our hosting provider records standard server information such as IP address, browser type, pages viewed, and the time of your visit.
  • Advertising cookies. We use the Meta Pixel (Facebook and Instagram) on this site. It sets cookies and sends Meta information about your visit, such as the pages you view and whether you tap a quote or call button. Meta uses this so we can measure our ads and show Lumen 514 ads to people who visited this site. Meta's own handling of that data is described in the Meta Privacy Policy.

How we use it

To respond to your request, prepare your quote, perform the work you hire us for, follow up about your project, and improve our website and advertising. We never sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

Service providers that help us run the business: our website host, our form and email providers, Meta for advertising measurement, and the install crews who perform installations. Each receives only what it needs to do its job. We also share information when the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Opt out of Meta's ad personalization in your Facebook ad preferences, or block cookies in your browser settings.
  • Ask us to see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you by emailing josh@lumen514.com.
  • Reply STOP to any text from us to end text messages.
