What is the soffit and fascia made of?

Vinyl, aluminum, wood, cement fiber, thin cement board. Thin board (about 1/4 in) means screw-through-track and the customer has to OK visible screws.

Multiple soffit materials, needing different tracks?

A front with deep fascia and a porch with no eave can mean U track and Back track on one job.

Track type per section

U: deep fascia, flush with the bottom edge. Flap: shallow fascia, metal or vinyl soffit. Back: flat surface, no eave.

How many soffit and fascia colors?

Each color may need its own track color.

Track color picked with the swatch, in daylight

Hold the swatch flat against the fascia. Customer agrees out loud.

Will loom or wire show anywhere?

Walk every transition. Downspouts first, trim second, attic beats both. Point to each spot and get the OK now.

Do we need conduit?

Long exterior wire runs with no trim or downspout to hide behind.

Inside attic, garage, Wi-Fi

Attic access for power and wire?

Attic routes keep every transition hidden. Find the hatch and go up.

Can we reach the corners and peaks from the attic?

Flat soffit runs show daylight from inside. Peaks need a hole through the 2x6 at the top.

Where is the outlet for the controller?

Garage or attic. Controller mounts within 6 ft of it. Own circuit, never shared with the garage door opener or an EV charger. Reachable by the homeowner.

Is the Wi-Fi close?

Stand where the controller will mount and check your phone. Weak signal means an extender on the quote.

Wi-Fi band: 2.4 GHz available, or 5 GHz only?

The Bosso controller only joins 2.4 GHz. Ask the homeowner to open their router app. One combined network is usually fine. 5 GHz only means they split the bands or add a 2.4 network before install day, or the app will never pair.
