FAQ

Asked and answered.

Can you see the lights during the day?

Barely. The low-profile track is color-matched to your roofline from 19 colors and tucks under the drip edge. From the street your home looks unchanged until dark.

How long do the lights last?

Commercial-grade LEDs rated for 50,000+ hours, which is decades of normal use, built for sun, snow, wind, and rain, and covered by the warranty.

Is the app hard to use?

If you can send a text, you can run your lights. Pick a color, tap a preset, or set a schedule once and never think about it again. We set up your first scenes before we leave.

Can I make my own patterns?

Yes. Unlimited custom presets, per-zone colors and brightness, animations, and one-tap looks for any occasion.

What if something goes wrong?

Most issues are fixed remotely through the app's cloud access. If hardware ever fails, it's warrantied and serviceable by section, and you deal directly with the owner.
